Full mouth dental implants replace all missing teeth in the upper arch, lower arch or both, using a set of implants placed in the jaw to support a fixed set of new teeth.

All-on-4 uses four implants to support a full arch, while All-on-6 uses six. The right option depends on your jawbone, bite and goals, and we review this during your evaluation.

Many eligible patients can receive immediate load dental implants with temporary teeth the same day. We confirm whether this is right for you after an exam and imaging.
